# /gen-video

Invoke the `media-creative-director` subagent and `ai-video-generation` skill.

## Capabilities

1. **AI Video Prompt Directing**:
   - Choreograph camera vectors (`dolly in`, `truck left`, `pedestal up`, `orbit 180`).
   - Specify kinematic physical actions (`60fps`, `slow-motion fluid drape`, `atmospheric particles`).
   - Optimize for Runway Gen-3, OpenAI Sora, Kling AI, or Luma Dream Machine.
2. **Automated FFmpeg Recipes**:
   - Lossless trimming and multi-clip concatenation.
   - Dynamic audio normalization (`loudnorm`) and track replacement.
   - Conversion to web-optimized H.264/AAC.
3. **Remotion Code-Driven Video**:
   - Write React components that render automated, 60fps dynamic product video reels.
